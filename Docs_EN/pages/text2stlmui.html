Text2STLMUI

Purpose

Text2STLMUI creates simple text-based STL objects directly on the Amiga.

Text Lines

The program supports up to three text lines. The lines are centered in the STL.

Modes

PIXELBuilt-in 5x7 pixel style. Very readable and good for printing.
AMIGAUses selected Amiga fonts. Some fonts print better than others.

Case

The text can be kept as typed, forced uppercase, or forced lowercase depending on the option selected in the interface.

Output

The generated ASCII STL can be viewed with 3DView, sent to SlicerMUI, then printed through OctoControlSpeak.
