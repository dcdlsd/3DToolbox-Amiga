3DToolbox Amiga

3DToolbox groups several Amiga tools around 3D printing: OctoPrint control, PrusaSlicer through a PC server, STL files, G-code, color-change pauses, monitoring and Z setup.

The Amiga remains the control station. The PC or Raspberry Pi only perform the modern or heavy tasks that are not practical on a classic Amiga.
